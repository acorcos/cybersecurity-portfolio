\# Internal Security Audit - Botium Toys



\## Overview



This project presents an internal security audit for \*\*Botium Toys\*\*, a fictional company used as part of the Google Cybersecurity Certificate.



The goal of this activity is to assess the organization’s current security posture by reviewing its assets, identifying risks, evaluating existing controls, and checking compliance best practices.



This project demonstrates practical understanding of security auditing, risk assessment, security controls, compliance requirements, and the NIST Cybersecurity Framework.



\## Scenario



Botium Toys is a small U.S.-based company that develops and sells toys. The company has a physical location that includes its main office, storefront, and warehouse. It also has a growing online presence, attracting customers both in the United States and internationally.



As the business grows, the IT department needs to improve security, maintain compliance, and reduce risks related to data protection, online payments, and business continuity.



\## Audit Scope



The audit scope includes the entire security program at Botium Toys, including:



\- Employee equipment and devices

\- On-premises equipment

\- Internal network

\- Systems, software, and services

\- Internet access

\- Data storage and retention

\- Legacy systems

\- Storefront and warehouse assets



\## Audit Goals



The main goals of this audit are to:



\- Assess existing IT assets

\- Identify missing or weak security controls

\- Review administrative, technical, and physical controls

\- Evaluate compliance with relevant standards and regulations

\- Provide recommendations to improve the organization’s security posture



\## Frameworks and Concepts Used



This audit uses concepts from:



\- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)

\- CIA Triad: Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability

\- Administrative, technical, and physical controls

\- PCI DSS

\- GDPR

\- SOC Type 1 and SOC Type 2

\- Least privilege

\- Separation of duties

\- Defense in depth

\- Risk assessment



\## Key Findings



The audit identified several security gaps:



\- The overall risk score is high: \*\*8/10\*\*

\- All employees have access to internally stored data

\- Employees may be able to access customers’ PII/SPII and cardholder data

\- Least privilege has not been implemented

\- Separation of duties has not been implemented

\- Encryption is not used to protect credit card data

\- No intrusion detection system (IDS) is installed

\- No disaster recovery plan is in place

\- Critical data is not backed up

\- Password requirements are weak

\- No centralized password management system is used

\- Legacy systems are monitored, but there is no regular schedule or clear intervention process



Some controls are already in place, including:



\- Firewall

\- Antivirus software

\- Physical locks

\- CCTV surveillance

\- Fire detection and prevention systems

\- GDPR breach notification plan



\## Recommendations



Botium Toys should prioritize the following actions:



\- Implement least privilege access controls

\- Apply separation of duties

\- Strengthen password policies

\- Deploy a centralized password management system

\- Implement encryption for credit card and sensitive customer data

\- Install an intrusion detection system (IDS)

\- Create and test a disaster recovery plan

\- Implement regular backups of critical data

\- Improve asset classification and inventory

\- Review access to PII/SPII and cardholder data

\- Establish regular monitoring and maintenance procedures for legacy systems



\## Conclusion



This audit shows that Botium Toys has some basic security controls in place, but several important gaps remain. By implementing the recommended controls and improving compliance practices, the organization can reduce risk, better protect sensitive data, and strengthen its overall security posture.



\## Disclaimer



This project is based on a fictional scenario provided for educational purposes as part of cybersecurity training. It does not represent a real company audit.
